About Me

I am a geospatial professional specializing in GIS, remote sensing, and environmental analysis. My interests include landscape change detection, conservation, wildlife disease ecology, disaster resilience, and the application of geospatial technologies to environmental challenges. I have a Master of Applied Science in Geospatial Information Technologies and a Bachelor of Science in Geographic Information Sciences with a minor in Environmental Science. I enjoy using spatial data to solve real-world problems. I have experience with ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, ENVI, remote sensing workflows, drone photogrammetry, Python, R and spatial analysis.

Featured Research

Landscape Change Detection and Chronic Wasting Disease

My thesis research examines the relationship between landscape change and the spread of Chronic Wasting Disease across Louisiana and Mississippi. The project integrates GIS, remote sensing, vegetation change detection, spatial analysis, and environmental data to explore patterns associated with disease occurrence and landscape conditions.
