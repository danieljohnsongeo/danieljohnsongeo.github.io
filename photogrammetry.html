Project Overview

This project used drone imagery and photogrammetry processing to create geospatial products for evaluating visibility from a deer stand location. The workflow included aerial image collection with a DJI Mini Pro 4, orthophoto generation, elevation surface modeling, 3D visualization in WebODM, 3D visulization in ArcGIS pro and visibility analysis.

The project demonstrates applied GIS, UAV-based mapping, elevation analysis, and the use of photogrammetry outputs to support site-level spatial decision-making.
