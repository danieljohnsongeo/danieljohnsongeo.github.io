About Me

I am a geospatial professional specializing in GIS, remote sensing, and environmental analysis. My interests include landscape change detection, conservation, wildlife disease ecology, disaster resilience, and the application of geospatial technologies to environmental challenges. I have a Master of Applied Science in Geospatial Information Technologies and a Bachelor of Science in Geographic Information Sciences with a minor in Environmental Science. I enjoy using spatial data to solve real-world problems. I have experience with ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, ENVI, remote sensing workflows, drone photogrammetry, Python, R and spatial analysis.

Featured Projects

Chronic Wasting Disease Landscape Analysis

Thesis research examining the relationship between landscape change, vegetation patterns, and the spread of Chronic Wasting Disease across Louisiana and Mississippi.

Drone Photogrammetry and Visibility Analysis

A field-based GIS project using DJI Mini 4 Pro imagery, photogrammetry processing, elevation products, and line-of-sight analysis to evaluate visibility around a deer stand.

Hurricane Vegetation Change Detection

Remote sensing analysis using pre- and post-event imagery to evaluate vegetation change and potential storm impacts through NDVI-based methods.

Web GIS and ArcGIS Applications
